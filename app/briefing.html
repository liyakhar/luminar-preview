Vaswani et al., 2017

Attention Is All You Need

BriefStandardDeepEnglish▶ ListenExport PDFShare

In short

The paper introduces the Transformer, a model for translation that drops recurrence and convolutions and relies only on attention.1 It trains faster than earlier models and set new records on two translation benchmarks.2

Key points

  • Attention lets every word look at every other word directly, so the model can be trained in parallel instead of one step at a time.1
  • Multi-head attention runs several attention layers side by side, so the model can follow different kinds of relations at once.3
  • Because there is no recurrence, word order is added with positional encodings.4
  • It reached 28.4 BLEU on English to German and 41.8 BLEU on English to French, after 3.5 days of training on eight GPUs.2
  • The same model also worked well for English constituency parsing, a sign it is useful beyond translation.5
