Questions

Which YouTube videos work?

Most videos with speech work. Luminar reads the spoken words, so a video with no speech or no transcript may not work.

How long can the video be?

Most YouTube videos have subtitles, and Luminar reads them, so even a three-hour video works. On paid plans there is no hour limit for those. Videos without subtitles are listened to instead: up to 30 minutes each on the free plan, and 30 or 100 hours a month on paid plans.

Can I check the summary against the video?

Yes. Each point is cited. Tap the number to see where in the source it came from.

Do I need the extension?

No. The web app works from a link. The Chrome extension adds a Summarize button right under YouTube videos.

Can Luminar follow a YouTube channel for me?

Yes. Follow a channel and every new video is summarized into your library. Turn on the daily email to get one short email a day with each new video, so you can decide in seconds what is worth watching.

Is it free?

Yes, you can start free with no card. The free plan includes 5 summaries a month, YouTube included. Up to 2 of them can be audio files or videos without subtitles, up to 30 minutes each. Paid plans start at $8 a month billed annually, with no hour limit for YouTube videos that have subtitles.
