Questions

Does it work with arXiv?

Yes. Paste an arXiv link and Luminar reads the paper.

Will it explain the jargon?

The briefing is written in plain words, so you can follow the main idea even outside your field. Check the original for exact definitions.

How many papers can I summarize?

The free plan covers research articles within its 5 summaries a month. Pro includes 50 research articles a month and Power includes 100.

Should I cite the summary?

No. Use it to understand and find the right passages, then cite the paper itself. Every point in the briefing links to its source to make that easy.

Is it free?

Yes, you can start free with no card. The free plan includes 5 summaries a month, YouTube included. Up to 2 of them can be audio files or videos without subtitles, up to 30 minutes each. Paid plans start at $8 a month billed annually, with no hour limit for YouTube videos that have subtitles.
