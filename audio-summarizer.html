What you get with every briefing

A briefing is more than a block of text. It is short, it shows where each point came from, and it stays with you.

Every point cited

Each point in the briefing links back to the part of the source it came from, so you can check it in one tap.

Saved to your library

Briefings stay in your library. Sort them into collections and come back to them later.

Ask follow-up questions

Ask anything about the briefing. Answers come only from that source and show where they came from.

Listen instead

Share or download

Questions

How long can a recording be?

On the free plan, audio and video summaries can be up to 30 minutes each, 2 a month. Pro includes 30 hours a month and Power includes 100 hours.

What if there is no speech?

Luminar summarizes what is said, so music or silence will not give a useful briefing.

Is my recording private?

Only the file you choose to summarize is sent, to our servers and AI providers, to make your briefing. Your reading is never sold.

Is it free?

Yes, you can start free with no card. The free plan includes 5 summaries a month, YouTube included. Up to 2 of them can be audio files or videos without subtitles, up to 30 minutes each. Paid plans start at $8 a month billed annually, with no hour limit for YouTube videos that have subtitles.
