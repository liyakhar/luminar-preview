Questions

Which PDFs work?

PDFs that contain real text work best. A scan that is only a picture of pages may not work, because there is no text to read.

Can I trust the summary?

Every point is cited, so you can tap it and see the part of the PDF it came from. For important decisions, always check the original.

Is my file private?

Only the file you choose to summarize is sent, to our servers and AI providers, to make your briefing. Your reading is never sold. See our privacy policy for details.

Can I download the summary?

Yes. You can download any briefing as a PDF or share it with a link.

Is it free?

Yes, you can start free with no card. The free plan includes 5 summaries a month, YouTube included. Up to 2 of them can be audio files or videos without subtitles, up to 30 minutes each. Paid plans start at $8 a month billed annually, with no hour limit for YouTube videos that have subtitles.
